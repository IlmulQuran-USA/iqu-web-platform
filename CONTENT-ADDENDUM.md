## 2. Policy facts that change site copy (live, Tuition & Refund Policy, 16 Nov 2026)

These come from the published policy, so they outrank older drafts:

- **First month free:** new students study their first month free. The first payment is taken when the free month ends, with an email reminder before that date. Nothing is charged if the family stops before the free month ends.
- **Payment methods:** US bank account (ACH) or debit/credit card, through Stripe.
- **Cancelled classes:** classes cancelled by the teacher or by us and not made up are refunded, or taken off the next month. Use this in place of the "free make-up sessions" claim. Never promise make-ups beyond what the policy says.
- **Stopping:** give at least 7 days' notice before the billing date. A full refund is given if the family tells us within 48 hours of a charge and no class has taken place that month.
- **Fee burden (live, quote as written):** "No child's place is ever affected by cost."
- **Late fees:** none.

## 3. Courses — gaps filled

### 3.1 Islamic Studies (draft)

**Overview:** Islamic Studies gives children, teens and adults a clear, practical grounding in their faith: what Muslims believe, how to worship correctly and how to live with good character. It is taught by qualified ‘Ulama and Muftis.

**Who it's for:** anyone who wants structured Islamic knowledge alongside their Qur’an classes. Lessons are pitched to the student's age and level.

**Topics:**
- **Aqeedah:** the six pillars of Iman and Tawheed.
- **Fiqh of worship:** purification, wudu, salah, fasting, zakah and Hajj.
- **Seerah:** the life of the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Stories:** the Prophets and the Sahabah.
- **Akhlaq and Adab:** character and manners.
- **Daily life:** duas and adhkar.
- **Advanced students:** selected topics in Hadith and Tafsir.

### 3.3 Course FAQs (draft — for every course page)

**Can I choose a male or female teacher?**
Male teachers teach brothers; female teachers teach sisters and young children.

**One-on-one or group?**
Both. Choose one-on-one or a small batch when you enroll.

**What if a class is cancelled?**
If your teacher or we cancel a class and it isn't made up, it is refunded or taken off your next month (Tuition Policy).

**Is there a certificate?**
Yes, at the end of the course.

**Can we change days or the course later?**
Yes. The new monthly amount starts from your next billing date, and we confirm it with you first (Tuition Policy).

### 3.4 How we match a teacher (draft, from About and the course rules)

We match by the student's level, the teacher gender the family prefers (female teachers for girls and young children), and the days and times that suit the family.
