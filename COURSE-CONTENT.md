## 4. Quran Nazirah (reading the Quran)

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Live URL | `/course/quran-nazirah/` |

| Priced as | `qaidah` (the same rate as Noorani Qaidah) |
| Meta (live) | "Learn to recite the Quran fluently with correct Tajweed. Online Nazirah classes for brothers and sisters of all ages." |

**Overview (live)**
Online Quran Nazirah (recitation) classes for brothers and sisters of all ages. Students develop fluency in reading the Quran with proper Tajweed, guided by qualified male and female teachers.

**Where it fits (owner):** Nazirah is reading the Quran itself, after the Qaidah and before Hifz.

**Who it's for:** students who have completed the Noorani Qaidah or can already read Arabic letters with harakat.

**Highlights (live)**
- Fluent reading of the Quran with correct Tajweed
- Tajweed rules: proper articulation and pronunciation
- One-on-one or group classes, on your schedule
- Male teachers for brothers; female teachers for sisters and young children
- Learn from home, at a time that suits you

**How it is taught (draft — confirm)**
1. **Bridging from the Qaidah:** reading short surahs of Juz ‘Amma from the mushaf, applying the rules learned in the Qaidah.
2. **Reading through the Quran:** the student reads aloud to the teacher, portion by portion, juz by juz. The teacher listens, corrects pronunciation and Tajweed on the spot, and sets the next portion.
3. **Fluency and completion:** steady pace, correct stops (waqf) and Tajweed applied without prompting, working toward a complete reading (khatm) of the Quran.
4. **Revision:** earlier portions are re-read regularly so fluency holds.

**Overview (live)**
For students who wish to memorize the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed and fluency. A structured approach lets each student memorize at their own pace under the supervision of qualified teachers. One-on-one and batch classes are available.

**Who it's for:** students who can read the Quran fluently (Nazirah level).

**Highlights (live)**
- 100% online, with flexible scheduling
- A personal memorization plan based on each student's capability
- Emphasis on Tajweed and correct pronunciation
- Daily revision and retention techniques
- Experienced male and female teachers
- One-on-one and batch options
- Suitable for children and adults

**How it is taught (PRODUCT.md method + draft — confirm)**

Every class has three parts:
- **Sabaq:** the new lesson. A set number of lines, memorized and recited to the teacher.
- **Sabqi:** revision of the recent lessons, so new memorization settles.
- **Manzil:** revision of older portions, so nothing memorized is lost.

Path *(draft — confirm)*:
- The student usually starts with Juz ‘Amma, then moves through the Quran juz by juz.
- The daily amount grows with the student's ability.
- Teachers set homework revision between classes.

**Certificate:** yes.

**Overview (live)**
Master the fundamentals of Quranic Arabic. The program combines grammar, vocabulary and practical Quranic application to build a deep, lasting connection with the Book of Allah, in sha’ Allah.

**Objective (live):** to understand at least 80% of Quranic verses through listening and reading within one year, in sha’ Allah. Present this as the course's aim, never as a guarantee.

**Who it's for (live):** students who can read the Quran fluently with harakat and sukun. No prior Arabic grammar is needed.

**Highlights (live)**
- Practical, Quran-focused learning
- Guided grammar and vocabulary
- Weekly translation and comprehension practice
- A final tafsir and grammar project

**Curriculum (live)**

**Phase 1 — Foundations (months 1–3)**
- *Goal:* core grammar and vocabulary for basic Quranic structures.
- *Topics:*
  - the alphabet (refresher)
  - ism, fi‘l and harf
  - definite and indefinite (al, tanween)
  - personal and attached pronouns
  - gender and number
  - present and past tense
  - jumlah ismiyyah and fi‘liyyah
  - mubtada’–khabar
  - root-based vocabulary
- *Practice:* short Quranic phrases, Juz ‘Amma vocabulary, simple translation and tafsir exercises.

**Phase 2 — Grammar expansion (months 4–6)**
- *Goal:* mid-level sentence structures and essential grammar.
- *Topics:*
  - detailed pronoun use
  - verb forms I–V
  - harf al-jarr
  - idafah
  - adjectives
  - negation (la, lam, lamma, ma, laysa)
  - inna
  - common Quranic particles
- *Practice:* short ayahs from Juz 30 and 29, 500 common Quranic words, root extraction.

**Phase 3 — Advanced grammar and Quranic style (months 7–9)**
- *Goal:* Quranic expressions and complex grammar.
- *Topics:*
  - verb forms I–X
  - conditionals and relative clauses
  - kana and its sisters
  - inna and its sisters
  - Quranic emphasis
  - an introduction to balaghah
- *Practice:* ayah analysis, longer passages, comparative meaning analysis.

**Phase 4 — Full Quranic immersion (months 10–12)**
- *Goal:* direct comprehension and analysis of Quranic verses.
- *Topics:*
  - Al-Fatihah
  - excerpts from Al-Baqarah, Yasin, Al-Mulk and Al-Kahf
  - thematic vocabulary
  - integrated recitation and understanding
  - tafsir-based learning
  - listening comprehension
- *Practice:* weekly translation assignments, oral quizzes, listening tests, student presentations.

**Assessment (live)**
- Monthly quizzes
- Mid-course exam at 6 months
- Final grammar and tafsir project on a selected surah
- Final exam in translation and listening comprehension

**Outcomes (live, in sha’ Allah)**
- Understand about 80% of the Quranic text through listening and reading
- Translate and analyze a wide range of Quranic passages
- A foundation for advanced study

**Note (live):** Ilm-Ul-Quran USA may adjust the curriculum to better meet students' needs.

**Certificate:** yes.

**Teachers (live page):** Mufti Muaj Al-Juhany, Mawlana Nazrul Islam.
